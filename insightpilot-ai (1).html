Data Quality Score

Upload a dataset to calculate its quality score

How is this score calculated?

The score starts at 100 and loses points for each kind of problem. Every number comes from your current data, and each penalty has a cap so one issue can't zero the score on its own.

  • Missing values (up to 30 pts): 1.5 pts for every 1% of cells that are blank or a placeholder such as "N/A", "null" or "-".
  • Duplicate rows (up to 20 pts): 2 pts for every 1% of rows that exactly repeat an earlier row.
  • Inconsistent text (up to 20 pts): 2 pts for every 1% of text cells whose spelling, capitalisation or spacing differs from the most common form of the same value.
  • Outliers (up to 15 pts): 3 pts for every 1% of numeric cells beyond 1.5 × the interquartile range. Outliers are not always mistakes, so they carry a smaller weight.
  • Incorrect data types (up to 15 pts): 1.5 pts for every 1% of all cells holding numbers or dates stored as text.

Any remaining issue keeps the score at 99 or below. Grades: 90+ Excellent, 75+ Good, 50+ Needs work, below 50 Poor. Mixed-type columns and ID-like columns are listed as notes but are never changed automatically.

Fixes never run automatically. Each one shows a preview first, and every applied fix can be undone. Your originally uploaded data is kept separately, so "Restore original" always works.
